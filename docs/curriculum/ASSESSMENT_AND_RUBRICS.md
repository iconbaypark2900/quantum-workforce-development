# Assessment and rubrics

The month is **competency-based**. Each week is pass / revise / fail. Revising within 7 days of feedback is expected.

## Week 1 — Learning plan (10 points)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| Primary pathway named and matches the Track result *or* a justified override | Missing | Named, weak fit | Named, evidence from SME |
| Stretch pathway is adjacent, not a fantasy title | Missing | Unrelated stretch | Honest adjacent |
| 30-day artifact is observable | Vague | Activity, not artifact | Concrete brief / card / memo |
| Non-goal is specific | Missing | Generic humility | Clear boundary |
| Honesty about math/code gaps | Inflated | Partial | Matches Track answers |

**Pass:** ≥ 7 and Track completed.

## Week 2 — Qubit card + check (10 points)

Concept check: 10 items, 1 point each. Automatic revise if superposition / entanglement / interference / measurement are swapped.

Qubit card (holistic 0–2, scaled into remaining judgment): four definitions in the learner’s words plus one retired misconception.

**Pass:** check ≥ 7 **and** no pillar mix-up **and** card complete.

## Week 3 — Use-case memo (10 points)

| Criterion | 0 | 2 | 3–4 |
|-----------|---|---|-----|
| Problem is a use case, not an industry label | Industry only | Named problem | Owner + metric |
| Classical status acknowledged | Ignored | Mentioned | Why classical is hard *or* sufficient |
| IBM “good vs poor” factors applied | None | One factor | Strategic, scalable, hybrid-near-term |
| Honesty about NISQ / timeline | Hype | Cautious | Explicit limits |
| Writing | Unreadable | Uneven | 400 words, cite IBM lesson |

Scale the rows so the memo totals 10. Badge is recorded separately and does not replace the memo.

**Pass:** ≥ 7.

## Week 4 — Capstone brief (20 points)

| Criterion | Points | Fail if |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Vocabulary appendix: ≥ 12 core terms, no circular defs | 4 | Slogans (“it’s magic”) |
| Classical baseline: problem, instance, method, metric, threshold | 5 | Any of the five missing |
| Mapping: encoding or explicit “does not map” | 3 | Hand-wave to “the quantum computer” |
| Optimization notes: method, backend, shots/iters, noise | 3 | Unlabeled simulator-as-hardware |
| Comparison table + go/no-go with reason | 5 | Adjectives instead of numbers |

**Pass:** ≥ 14 **and** baseline section complete. A no-go recommendation can be a full pass.

## Certificate of completion

Issued when all four weeks pass. IBM badge is additional, not required for the QGG month certificate.
